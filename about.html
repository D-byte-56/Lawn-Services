Who we are

One crew. Diferent services. No handoffs.

EV Lawncare GA Inc has spent twelve years working properties across Forsyth and Dawson County — dropping trees, grinding stumps, building walls, running irrigation lines, and setting fence posts in Georgia clay.

Most outdoor jobs need more than one trade. A tree comes down, the stump has to go, then the slope needs a wall, then the sod needs water. Running all of that through one crew means one schedule, one point of contact, and one company standing behind the finished result.

Our equipment
Our equipment

The right equipment for the job.

We run our own equipment, and we bring the machine the job actually calls for — not whatever happens to be on the trailer. Nothing gets improvised and nothing gets handed off to a subcontractor. The crew that shows up owns the gear and knows how to run it.

The right machine does the work faster, safer, and with less damage to your property — fewer ruts across the lawn, less risk when a tree comes down close to the house, and a cleaner site when we leave. The wrong one tears up the yard or can't finish the job.

We keep the equipment maintained, because a machine that quits halfway through is your delay, not just ours. And since the same crew runs everything from the first pass of clearing to the finished yard, the work moves start to finish without waiting on anyone else.
