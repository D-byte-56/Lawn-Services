Who we are

One crew. Five trades. No handoffs.

EV Lawncare GA Inc has spent twelve years working properties across Forsyth and Dawson County — dropping trees, grinding stumps, building walls, running irrigation lines, and setting fence posts in Georgia clay.

Most outdoor jobs need more than one trade. A tree comes down, the stump has to go, then the slope needs a wall, then the sod needs water. Running all of that through one crew means one schedule, one point of contact, and one company standing behind the finished result.

Every estimate is free and given in writing before anything starts.
